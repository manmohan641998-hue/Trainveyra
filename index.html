TrainVeyra — Train Status, Live Radar, PNR & Seat Availability

TrainVeyra is a fast, mobile-first railway information platform built on live data from the RailRadar API.

  • Search Indian trains by number or name.
  • Live train radar, running status and station-wise journey progress.
  • Live PNR status and seat availability lookup.
  • SEO-ready metadata, canonical URL, structured data and descriptive content.
